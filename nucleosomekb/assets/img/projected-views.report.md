## Flags

- **Tail extent is the drawing's, not the projection's.** Blended tails are reparameterized onto the drawn tube's own length. The real MD-average tails are far longer than the drawn stubs, so walking them at true projected spacing threw most of every tail outside the viewBox. This is a departure from a literal reading of spec 5.4 and needs ratifying; it applies to the stylized layer only - the core is projected exactly.
- **No position may be read from padded nodes.** They are coincident with the nearest resolved residue and exist so that node i is residue i in every stroke.
- **The DNA is fitted without correspondence.** The drawn segments are not in path order and several run reversed, so no arc-length correspondence is used; the pose comes from a rigid camera plus trimmed nearest-neighbour matching, and the anisotropy is released afterwards with the view direction held fixed.
- **Cartoon tails are stroked, not outlined.** The drawn tails were authored as a constant-width stroke converted to an outline; the animation simply does not repeat the conversion, emitting each tail as a black path at w + 2*0.176 under a coloured path at w, both morphing one `d`. Width is then exactly constant with no offset geometry to self-intersect. w is RECOVERED per view from area and perimeter (pi w^2/4 - wP/2 + A = 0): topview 0.964 (sd 0.047), sideview 1.481 (sd 0.020) across all ten tails. Centerline is a rolling mean of 9 residues (params: animation.smooth).
- **The trajectory determines the tail extent** [BB 2026-08-07] - no rescaling onto the drawn stub. The earlier compression answered a measurement error: arc length said the real tails were ~2x too long, but on REACH the drawn and projected tails already agree (median 0.86 topview, 1.02 sideview).
- **Tails are attached to the fold.** The boundary residue lands where the projection puts it, which is not inside the drawn fold, so an unattached tail starts in mid-air. Exactly one node is prepended - the crossing of the fold outline along the tail's own backward direction, or the nearest point on the outline if that ray misses - which keeps the node count constant across frames.
- **NOT YET ALIGNED.** The drawn folds are not where the projected folds are (HFD centroid residual 7.7 A topview, 10.5 A sideview; BB's hand-nudges run to 17 A). A projection/cartoon alignment pass is pending and no offsets are baked in. Read tail attachment points as provisional until it lands.
- **The HFD is not animated.** Core RMSF is 0.59 A median, below the line width; animating it would invent motion.
- **The animated variant is pure projection** - no blend, no routing onto artwork, and all eight strokes moved to the FOREGROUND so the motion is legible over the cartoon. It is a separate asset (`*-animated.svg`); the static `-projected` files keep the in-slot paint order.
- **The loop is forward-only** [BB 2026-08-07], over 42 consecutive frames of the Armeev trajectory at 100 ns spacing. The earlier ping-pong was the source of the synchronised tail breathing: played backwards every tail necessarily retraces, so they all expand and contract together. In the trajectory itself the tails are independent (median pairwise r = -0.06 between tail radii of gyration). Interpolation between keyframes is linear, so intermediate poses are morphs, not sampled conformations.
- **Frame 0 is excluded.** It is the symmetrised starting model - both copies of every histone are identical in it, which no thermally sampled frame is - and it is not a sample.
- **HFD centroid residuals are several A** because the drawn fold shapes are cartoons - in the sideview the same blob is reused for different seats (H3:1/H4:1 share area and axes exactly), so they carry position but no per-seat orientation.
